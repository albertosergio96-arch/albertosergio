Weather-Induced Power Plant Outages: Empirical Evidence from Hydro and Thermal Generators in Europe

Alberto Sergio and Francesco Pietro Colelli

Energy Economics, 148, 108549, 2025.

How does weather affect the reliability of European power plants?

Published article

Research summary

We combine power-plant outage records from ENTSO-E with local weather data across 20 European countries. Using econometric models, we examine how temperature and hydrological conditions affect outages at hydro and thermal generators. The study considers differences across generation technologies and between planned and forced outages, providing evidence on weather-related risks to electricity generation.

Under Strain: Projecting Climate-Induced Capacity Losses for European Thermal Power Plants

Alberto Sergio and Francesco Pietro Colelli

How could climate change affect thermal generation availability across Europe?

Research summary

We link empirical estimates of weather-related power-plant unavailability to CMIP6 climate projections. The analysis distinguishes the probability of an outage from the share of capacity lost when one occurs. We examine how projected losses vary across technologies, countries and seasons, and whether losses may occur simultaneously across countries. This matters for the availability of generation and the potential for neighbouring systems to provide support.

When Nuclear Falters: Electricity Trade, Gas Prices, and Energy Security in France

Alberto Sergio, Anna Alberini and Francesco Pietro Colelli

How did electricity trade shape the effects of France’s nuclear shortfall?

Research summary

We study France’s nuclear shortfall during the European energy crisis using hourly data on electricity prices, generation, demand and cross-border exchanges. The analysis combines instrumental-variable models with counterfactual scenarios and machine-learning predictions. We examine how trade helped France respond to reduced nuclear availability and how the effects were transmitted to neighbouring electricity markets, alongside changes in gas prices.

Dunkelflauten vs. Abundant Renewables: What Are the Effects on Electricity Prices and Trade?

Alberto Sergio and Anna Alberini

What happens to prices and electricity trade when wind and solar generation are scarce or abundant?

Research summary

We use hourly data across European electricity markets to examine periods of low and high wind and solar generation. The project studies how these conditions affect electricity prices and cross-border trade, and how the response depends on generation availability at home and in neighbouring markets. Particular attention is given to the role of interconnection and dispatchable generation during renewable scarcity.
