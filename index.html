About

I’m a PhD student in Economics at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences. My research focuses on energy economics, with a particular interest in electricity markets. I combine detailed energy datasets with econometric modelling and scenario analysis.

Following a publication in Energy Economics on weather-related power-plant outages, I extended this work by linking empirical models of generation availability to climate projections.

During a research visit to the University of Maryland in 2025–2026, I broadened my focus to energy security, studying how nuclear outages and periods of low or high renewable generation affect electricity prices, and the role of cross-border trade in responding to these changes.
