Welcome

I am a PhD student in Economics at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences, expecting to complete my PhD in April 2027. My research interests are energy economics, electricity markets and environmental economics.

Before starting my PhD, I earned an MSc in Economics from the University of Verona and a Master of Research in Science and Management of Climate Change from Ca’ Foscari University of Venice. I also worked at the Euro-Mediterranean Center on Climate Change (CMCC).

From August 2025 to April 2026, I was a visiting PhD researcher at the University of Maryland, where I worked on electricity prices, energy security and cross-border trade.

Research interests

  • Generation reliability Weather-related outages and climate risks to power generation.
  • Electricity markets Prices, renewable variability and nuclear availability.
  • Energy security Cross-border trade and simultaneous generation shortfalls.
